👩‍💻
A mum, a student, a migrant — in South Yarra

South Yarra, Melbourne VIC · Arrived March 2025

A year ago I landed in Melbourne by myself. My husband was still waiting for his visa. My daughter was with her grandparents overseas. I had a suitcase, a lot of hope, and absolutely no idea what I was doing. Two months later my husband arrived. A few months after that, our little one joined us. Now she's two, she's just started childcare, I'm studying a Master of Data Science, my husband works hard every single day, and we are — slowly, messily, beautifully — figuring out this life.
March 2025
I arrived in Melbourne alone. Just me and my suitcase and a whole lot of unknown ahead.
May 2025
My husband's visa came through. He arrived two months after me. We finally had each other in the same city again.
Late 2025
Our daughter joined us from overseas — after months of video calls, tears, and counting down the days. She was finally home.
Early 2026
She started childcare. I started my Master of Data Science. We moved to South Yarra. Life got real — and really good.
March 2026
One year in Melbourne. I started this blog to document what nobody else seems to be writing about: our life, exactly as it is.
I write because I kept searching for someone telling a story like mine — migrating with a young child, studying while parenting, living on one income in Melbourne, trying to belong somewhere new. I couldn't find it. So I'm writing it myself. If even one other mum reads this and feels less alone, that's everything.
My husband is a blue collar worker — he wakes up early, works hard, comes home tired, and still asks how my study went. He's the reason any of this is possible. Our daughter is 2 years old and has recently discovered both tantrums and the word "no." She is absolute chaos. She is our entire world. And me — I'm still figuring out who I am in this new country, this new city, this new chapter.
